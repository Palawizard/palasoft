## What it does

PalaClips keeps the last moments of your match in memory. A shortcut (Alt+F10 by default) saves them as a clip without leaving the game.

- Each clip has one audio track per app: the game, Discord, music, your mic. When editing, you can cut the music or the voice chat without touching the game sound.
- It can also record your screen (Ctrl+Alt+F9).
- A gallery lets you watch, trim and shrink your clips, for example under 20 MB to send them on Discord.
- It injects nothing into games and has been tested with Fortnite and its anti-cheat.
- No account to create, and it sends no usage data.
- It updates itself.

## Install

1. Download the file with the Download button, then open it.
2. It installs for your Windows account, no administrator password needed.
3. On first launch, PalaClips asks you a few questions one at a time (screen, mic, replay length, clips folder, shortcut). You can skip them: the default settings work very well.

Your clips are saved in your Videos folder, under PalaClips, with one folder per game.
