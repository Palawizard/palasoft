## What it does

Silence Splitter finds the silences in your MP4 videos and prepares the cut for your editing software.

- It sets the silence threshold by itself from your audio tracks, with four presets: action game, calm game, podcast and short video.
- You see and hear the result before exporting: the kept and cut parts show up on the waveform.
- It exports for DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere Pro, keeping your audio tracks separate.
- Your original videos are never changed.
- Everything happens on your PC, no account needed. The interface is in French and English, with a light or dark theme.
- It updates itself.

## Install

1. Download the file with the Download button, then open it.
2. It installs for your Windows account. Everything needed to read the videos is included, nothing else to install.

## Good to know

- This is a beta.
- It only reads MP4 videos.
