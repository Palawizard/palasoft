## What it does

Timestamper helps you mark the highlights of your Twitch stream while you play.

- A timer starts from zero when you start a session.
- A keyboard shortcut, usable from any app, notes the exact time since the start of the stream.
- After the stream, you find all your moments in the history. You can copy or export them (CSV or JSON).
- It can start and stop along with OBS, if you turn on the OBS WebSocket server. It never sends commands to OBS.
- Everything stays on your PC, offline, no account needed.

## Install

1. Download the file with the Download button, then open it.
2. It installs for your Windows account.
